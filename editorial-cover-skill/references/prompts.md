# 无字背景生成规范

输出给可用生图工具的 prompt 必须从文章语义生成，不能仅把标题粘贴进去。

## Prompt 模板

An understated editorial cover background about [ARTICULATED SUBJECT FROM ARTICLE].
Single focal subject: [ONE CONCRETE OBJECT], performing/showing [ONE CONCRETE ACTION OR CONTRAST].
Composition: [CENTER-ANCHORED FOR WECHAT / MID-CENTER WITH TOP TEXT ZONE FOR XHS / CENTER-WEIGHTED FOR DOUYIN].
Physical qualities: realistic materials, subtle natural light, documentary or restrained product photography, precise object geometry.
Visual language: quiet editorial, premium magazine composition, black and warm off-white with at most one muted accent color, broad intentional negative space, organic restrained imperfection, uncluttered frame.
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, watermark, logo, UI labels, CGI gloss, floating orbs, holograms, purple-blue gradients, cyberpunk neon, exaggerated depth of field, excessive reflections, fake brands, generic AI motifs.
Deliver image with [TARGET RATIO].

## 完整示例（不是强制默认画面）

主题：16GB Mac 上本地部署一个图片生成模型，单张耗时较长。
视觉重点：一台真实桌面电脑的简洁侧影，与一颗普通苹果的静物组合；不虚构品牌外观、不在画面生成计时文字。
构图：暖白纸质背景，苹果位于中心偏右，电脑画面仅露出一小角，留白较多。
语气：实验记录，非硬广。

## 拒绝 AI 模板化的检查语句

如果画面无法指明与文章正文相连的**具体物品或关系**，重做。
如果删掉光晕、渐变、悬浮小球之后画面便没有内容，重做。
如果海报的主要价值是上面的文字，那么考虑用排版主导，而非强行加装饰性生图。
