# Apple Marketing Guidelines — Relevant Rules

This reference summarizes only the Apple-product rules relevant to this Skill.

## Official source

Apple Developer — Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines:

https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/

## Relevant principles

### Apple product images

Apple states that Apple-provided product bezels should be used in marketing materials when displaying apps on Apple devices, and that Apple product images should be used as provided.

### Prohibited Apple product simulations

Apple's guidelines prohibit:

- rendering an Apple product in 3D for marketing materials
- creating a simulation of an Apple product
- illustrations that depict an Apple product, except for instructional material
- modifying Apple product images in prohibited ways

### Generic devices

If generic device illustrations are used, they should not reproduce details unique to Apple products, such as distinctive hardware controls or sensor designs.

### Association

Marketing materials must not falsely suggest an association with Apple or imply Apple endorsement.

### Copy

Marketing copy should focus on the app and its value, rather than making the Apple product itself the selling point.

Use correct Apple product names when they appear in copy.

## Important

This reference is not legal advice and is not a replacement for Apple's current guidelines.

Always check the official Apple page immediately before publishing marketing material.
